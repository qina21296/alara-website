Key Installation Steps

Step 01

Site Measurement

Accurately measure the roof area, slope angle, and purlin spacing before panel delivery. Verify all structural supports are level and properly anchored.

Step 02

Panel Unloading & Layout

Carefully unload panels and arrange them in installation sequence. Inspect each panel for any transit damage before lifting to the roof.

Step 03

Fixing & Fastening

Secure panels using self-drilling screws at purlin intersections. Maintain uniform overlap direction and apply sealant at all lap joints and ridge caps.

Step 04

Final Inspection

Conduct a full walkthrough inspection to verify panel alignment, screw torque, sealant application, and gutter/flashing connections before project sign-off.
