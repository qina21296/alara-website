Who We Are

Engineering the Future of Building Envelopes

ALARA New Materials is a technology-driven manufacturer specializing in high-performance metal roofing and cladding systems for the global construction industry.

Rooted in the engineering principle of ALARA — "As Low As Reasonably Achievable" — we engineer building envelopes that minimize structural risk while maximizing longevity. From high-impact weatherproof roof tiles to corrosion-resistant standing seam systems, every ALARA product is precision-engineered to perform under extreme conditions.

We don't just supply materials. We deliver lasting safety, stability, and peace of mind to architects, contractors, and developers worldwide.
